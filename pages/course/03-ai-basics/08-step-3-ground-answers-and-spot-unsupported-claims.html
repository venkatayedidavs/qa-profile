Step 3: Ground answers and spot unsupported claims

Why this lesson is here. Spot an unsupported claim against the shop quantity rule. The page says retrieval is not conflict resolution.

Before the exercise: read the quantity contract shown in Your practice lab and include that text, with its QTY IDs, in the request. No local file is needed yet; Step 5 creates the files.

Worked review: "Quantity 6 is valid because premium customers get a higher limit" is unsupported. "Quantity 5 is valid under QTY-01" is grounded. "What happens when stock is lower than quantity?" is a question, not a new requirement.

Practice: imagine retrieval returns an old rule allowing 10 and the current contract allowing 5. Identify the authoritative version before deciding an expected result. RAG is retrieval plus generation, not automatic conflict resolution.

Pass when: you keep observed behavior, agreed expectations, and assumptions separate.
