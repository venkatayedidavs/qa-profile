1.13 Reporters and artifacts

Why this lesson is here. What that run leaves behind: reporters, traces, and why tracing everything is expensive.

list and line for local runs, html for humans, junit for pipeline and test-management integration, blob for sharded runs which are then combined with merge-reports into one HTML report. Artifacts to configure deliberately: traces, screenshots and video, each with modes such as on-first-retry, only-on-failure and retain-on-failure. Tracing everything is expensive, so do not.
