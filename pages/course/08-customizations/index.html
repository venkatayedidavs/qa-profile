Why this module is here. AI steps 5 through 20. Instructions, one skill, the read-only QA reviewer, then a hook, each pointed at the shop tests.

What it unlocks. Defend the work. MCP and plugins come after a skill, a reviewer, and a hook.

Prerequisite: Phase 1. Outcome: run an independent check and make project guidance discoverable.

Prerequisite: a passing practice lab and understood instructions. Outcome: a reusable, evidence-based procedure with a tested discovery path.

Prerequisite: Phase 3. Outcome: a focused reviewer with a checked tool boundary and a human-controlled handoff.

Prerequisite: Phase 4 and a restored passing lab. Outcome: understand a lifecycle event, test the supplied script offline, and verify the matching live configuration. VS Code's hook experience is Preview; your organization may restrict it.

  1. Lesson 1 Step 5: Establish a working baseline

    The practice-lab files are created and the baseline test is run, before you ask an assistant to change anything.

  2. Lesson 2 Step 6: Review an AI suggestion like a tester

    Review a suggestion against a baseline you can already run without AI.

  3. Lesson 3 Step 7: Write repository instructions

    Repository instructions come after you have seen an unguided suggestion.

  4. Lesson 4 Step 8: Scope guidance; recognize saved prompts

    File-scoped guidance and saved prompts come after repository-wide instructions.

  5. Lesson 5 Step 9: Write the skill's procedure

    The procedure is written before supporting files. A skill is not a new model.

  6. Lesson 6 Step 10: Add one useful supporting resource

    One supporting resource is added only after the procedure exists.

  7. Lesson 7 Step 11: Invoke it and verify discovery

    Discovery is evidence the files are wired, which you cannot check before they exist.

  8. Lesson 8 Step 12: Evaluate the skill, including failures

    The phase gate evaluates the skill, including failures, after a real invocation.

  9. Lesson 9 Step 13: Create QA Reviewer

    The QA Reviewer is created before its boundary is tested.

  10. Lesson 10 Step 14: Prove the read-only boundary

    The page orders the read-only proof immediately after creation, before any handoff.

  11. Lesson 11 Step 15: Handoff versus subagent

    Handoff and subagent are compared after a reviewer role exists to hand work to.

  12. Lesson 12 Step 16: Connect QA review to development

    The phase gate connects review to a code change only after the tool boundary holds.

  13. Lesson 13 Step 17: Understand events, input, and decisions

    Events, input, and decisions come before the approval script.

  14. Lesson 14 Step 18: Create the approval script

    The supplied script comes after the event model, so you can read its decisions.

  15. Lesson 15 Step 19: Test before enabling

    The page's own order: offline tests before the hook is enabled.

  16. Lesson 16 Step 20: Verify live approval and disable cleanly

    The live check is last, and the lesson includes disabling the hook cleanly.
