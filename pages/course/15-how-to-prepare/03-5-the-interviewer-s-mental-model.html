5. The interviewer's mental model

Why this lesson is here. Five behaviours the plan says to aim every later answer at. They come before the answers.

An engineer with two solid Playwright years answers differently from a beginner in five specific ways. Aim every answer at these.

  1. They talk about locators and auto-waiting as a design contract with developers, not as syntax.
  2. They reach for fixtures and projects when asked about structure, not for base classes and inheritance.
  3. They treat flakiness as a diagnosable defect, name the category, and open the trace before touching the retry count.
  4. They can explain CI economics: workers, shards, browser downloads, run time, artifact size, and what they cut when the pipeline got slow.
  5. They hold opinions with trade-offs. "It depends" followed by the actual deciding factor is a senior answer; "it depends" alone is not.
