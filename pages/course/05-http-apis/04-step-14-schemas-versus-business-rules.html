Step 14: Schemas versus business rules

Progress is saved in this browser.

0%

Why this lesson is here. A schema that accepts the negative cases from the previous step would be a false pass.

Outcome: validate runtime JSON. A schema-valid price can be wrong; derive schemas from the contract, not app code.

Study: Zod parse and safeParse.

src/schemas/shop.ts

import { z } from 'zod';

export const catalogSchema = z.object({
  products: z.array(z.object({
    id: z.string(),
    name: z.string().min(1),
    priceCents: z.number().int().nonnegative(),
    inStock: z.boolean(),
  })),
});

export const cartSchema = z.object({
  id: z.string().uuid(),
  owner: z.enum(['alex', 'sam']),
  items: z.array(z.object({
    productId: z.string(),
    quantity: z.number().int().min(1).max(5),
    unitPriceCents: z.number().int().nonnegative(),
  })),
  totalCents: z.number().int().nonnegative(),
});

Pass when: demonstrate both schema and business failures.
