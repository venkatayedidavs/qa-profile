QA Automation with AI

One path from the Quality Assurance slides and the AI in Software slides. Testing and QA basics, then automation basics, then AI basics, then the first UI test. Later modules use the same slides as the work gets harder, with the AI material inside each stage. Each page links to the previous and next page.

The original single-file Quality Assurance and AI in Software pages remain published. A few lessons include a voiceover script beside the page, using wording already on the slide. There is no video.

One path

Testing and QA basics, then automation basics, then AI basics, then the first UI test. After that the same slides continue as the work gets harder, and the AI material stays inside each stage.

  1. Module 1 · 6 lessons

    Basics of testing and QA

    The opening of the Quality Assurance slides, the learning method, and the Practice Shop contract come before any code. Step 1 and the requirements and oracles work stay here.

    Unlocks: Basics of automation, after you can say what done means from the contract.

  2. Module 2 · 7 lessons

    Basics of automation

    Steps 2 through 5 and step 7. Setup, the lab server, and a look at the real page and the catalog request come before any test script. The phase-gate questions about a value, a type, and a promise stay here.

    Unlocks: Basics of AI, after the lab runs and the page has been looked at.

  3. Module 3 · 9 lessons

    Basics of AI

    Only the opening of the AI slides and AI steps 1 through 4. Model versus product versus your own check, a specific prompt, an unsupported claim against the shop quantity rule, and the decision table.

    Unlocks: The first UI test. You choose instruction, skill, agent, or hook here. You do not build one yet.

  4. Module 4 · 3 lessons

    First UI test written with AI

    Quality Assurance steps 8 through 10, immediately after the three basics. The first Playwright test uses the case and the page notes you already have, then a meaningful failure, then the AI review.

    Unlocks: HTTP and API. A browser test exists before a contract test.

  5. Module 5 · 5 lessons

    HTTP and API

    Quality Assurance steps 11 through 15. Complexity rises from the UI test to HTTP. Review a generated contract the way the AI slides say to review a suggestion.

    Unlocks: Reuse, after there are tests worth sharing.

  6. Module 6 · 5 lessons

    Reuse

    Steps 16 through 20. Fixtures and a page object come after the API tests. Repository instructions from the AI slides apply to that shared code.

    Unlocks: Quality and CI, after a suite exists.

  7. Module 7 · 5 lessons

    Quality and CI

    Steps 21 through 25. Extra browsers, stability, and CI come after a suite exists. Judge the assistant only after CI evidence exists.

    Unlocks: Customizations pointed at the shop tests.

  8. Module 8 · 16 lessons

    Customizations

    AI steps 5 through 20. Instructions, one skill, the read-only QA reviewer, then a hook, each pointed at the shop tests.

    Unlocks: Defend the work. MCP and plugins come after a skill, a reviewer, and a hook.

  9. Module 9 · 17 lessons

    Defend the work

    AI steps 21 through 24, then Quality Assurance steps 26 through 30. MCP and plugins last, then assessment, portfolio, and capstone.

    Unlocks: Nothing further on these two pages. The closing sections stay after the capstone, as on the original pages.
