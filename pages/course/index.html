QA Automation with AI

From manual QA to AI-assisted automation. The lessons are the notes already on this site: the Quality Assurance path, the AI in Software path, and the Playwright and TypeScript interview plan. Follow them in order. Each page says what it unlocks next.

Profile pages and job pages stay on the site home. They are application profiles, not lessons. Software Developer and Cloud Engineer are still placeholders, so they are not modules.

The original single-file Quality Assurance and AI in Software pages remain published. The interview plan remains in learn/. A few lessons include a voiceover script beside the page. There is no video.

Part 1. From manual testing to a portfolio

The Quality Assurance notes are a 30-step path for a tester who can already investigate and report defects, and is new to programming. Plain-language AI use starts in these steps. Skills, agents, and hooks come in Part 2.

  1. Module 1 · 6 lessons

    Start here

    The Quality Assurance page places the audience, the study rules, the contract, and the lab files before any numbered step, because later lessons test against that contract.

    Unlocks: Programming foundations, which assume you can already say what an assertion is and which repository you are writing into.

  2. Module 2 · 5 lessons

    Programming foundations

    Phase 1 of the Quality Assurance page. Its prerequisite is manual testing and basic computer use. Browser tests wait until you can explain a value, a type, and a promise.

    Unlocks: The first UI tests. The Phase 1 gate is the entrance to that module.

  3. Module 3 · 5 lessons

    First UI tests

    Phase 2 of the Quality Assurance page. Its prerequisite is the Phase 1 gate. The first Playwright test is written only after a case and a look at the real page.

    Unlocks: HTTP and API tests. The Phase 2 gate is the entrance to that module.

  4. Module 4 · 5 lessons

    HTTP and APIs

    Phase 3 of the Quality Assurance page. Its prerequisite is the Phase 2 gate. Contracts, business rules, and state come before fixtures and page objects.

    Unlocks: Reuse and integration. The Phase 3 gate is the entrance to that module.

  5. Module 5 · 5 lessons

    Reuse and integration

    Phase 4 of the Quality Assurance page. Its prerequisite is the Phase 3 gate. Fixtures and page objects are introduced only after there are tests worth reusing.

    Unlocks: Quality, CI, and release evidence. The Phase 4 gate is the entrance to that module.

  6. Module 6 · 5 lessons

    Quality and CI

    Phase 5 of the Quality Assurance page. Its prerequisite is the Phase 4 gate. Extra browsers, stability, and the pipeline come after a suite exists.

    Unlocks: Assessment and the portfolio write-up. The Phase 5 gate is the entrance to that module.

  7. Module 7 · 8 lessons

    Assessment and portfolio

    Phase 6 of the Quality Assurance page, plus the capstone, resource list, and extensions that the original page places after the steps. Its prerequisite is the Phase 5 gate.

    Unlocks: The AI customization path. Step 26 introduces reusable workflows; Part 2 is the depth behind that step.

Part 2. AI customizations you can check

The AI in Software notes follow the Quality Assurance learning method and reuse the Practice Shop quantity rule. They assume you can already tell a suggestion from evidence. The closing section points UI and API automation back at Part 1.

  1. Module 8 · 6 lessons

    AI basics, before you customize

    The AI page opens with outcomes, vocabulary, a decision table, and a practice lab before its 24 steps. The lab is a small extract of the Practice Shop contract from Part 1.

    Unlocks: Understand AI, the first phase, which asks you to use the decision table and the quantity rule.

  2. Module 9 · 4 lessons

    Understand AI

    Phase 1 of the AI page. Its prerequisite is basic computer use. The outcome is to explain the parts of a workflow and decide what evidence to trust, before any customization file.

    Unlocks: Context and instructions. The Phase 1 gate is the entrance to that module.

  3. Module 10 · 4 lessons

    Context and instructions

    Phase 2 of the AI page. Its prerequisite is Phase 1. Repository instructions are the smallest customization, and they come before a skill.

    Unlocks: Build a skill. The Phase 2 gate is the entrance to that module.

  4. Module 11 · 4 lessons

    Build a skill

    Phase 3 of the AI page. A skill is a procedure folder, ordered after instructions and before a custom agent.

    Unlocks: Build a custom agent. The Phase 3 gate is the entrance to that module.

  5. Module 12 · 4 lessons

    Build a custom agent

    Phase 4 of the AI page. A reviewer role with fewer tools comes after you have a skill to point it at, and before a hook that runs code.

    Unlocks: Build and test a hook. The Phase 4 gate is the entrance to that module.

  6. Module 13 · 4 lessons

    Build and test a hook

    Phase 5 of the AI page. The page says to understand events and decisions before enabling anything, and to test the script before it is turned on.

    Unlocks: Integrate and evaluate. The Phase 5 gate is the entrance to that module.

  7. Module 14 · 9 lessons

    Integrate and evaluate

    Phase 6 of the AI page, plus the closing sections the original page places after the steps. MCP and plugins come after you can already bound a skill, an agent, and a hook.

    Unlocks: Interview depth. You can write tests and you can explain the AI help around them, which is what the interview plan then asks you to defend.

Part 3. Interview depth in Playwright and TypeScript

The Playwright and TypeScript plan is interview depth for after you can write and diagnose tests. Its markdown source opens with working rules. The study page says to work through basics before the question bank. One section is a worked self-assessment for a specific resume; apply the same honesty rule to your own experience.

  1. Module 15 · 4 lessons

    How to prepare

    The plan's markdown source opens with the rules, the honest inventory, and the five behaviours interviewers listen for, before any syntax drill.

    Unlocks: Playwright basics. The study page says that section is the foundation the question bank rests on.

  2. Module 16 · 14 lessons

    Playwright basics you can say out loud

    The study page says to work through this section first. Each topic is the explanation, then a self-check an interviewer would expect in a sentence or two.

    Unlocks: The five-phase schedule, whose second phase tells you to study this section and then list the actionability checks unaided.

  3. Module 17 · 5 lessons

    Five phases with exit criteria

    The plan's tactical schedule. It says not to move on until the exit criterion is met, because those criteria are what interviewers test.

    Unlocks: The question bank. Phase 5's exit is that no question in the bank produces a blank pause.

  4. Module 18 · 11 lessons

    Interview question bank

    The study page collapses every answer so you can speak first and then compare. Phase 5 of the plan uses this bank for the mock interviews.

    Unlocks: The proof project and the rehearsal. The decisions in that project are the decisions these questions ask about.

  5. Module 19 · 7 lessons

    Proof project and rehearsal

    The plan places the artefact, the phrases to drop, the honest experience answer, the stories, and the rubric after the question spines exist.

    Unlocks: Nothing further in this course. The progress tracker is the list the plan says to copy into a private working file.
