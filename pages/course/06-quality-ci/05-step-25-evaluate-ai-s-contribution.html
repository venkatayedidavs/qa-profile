Step 25: Evaluate AI's contribution

Why this lesson is here. The phase gate measures an assistant only after deterministic checks and evidence exist.

Outcome: fault detection and human effort, not generated count. This is a learning experiment, not a universal productivity study.

Study: your red/green evidence and responsible use.

Evidence columns: variant, expected fault, result, detected/missed/false alarm, correction, effort, commit. A compile failure in the injected change is not business-defect detection.

Pass when: demonstrated detection and critical review, not merely green tests.
