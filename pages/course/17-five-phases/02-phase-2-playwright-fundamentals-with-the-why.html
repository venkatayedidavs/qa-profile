Phase 2 — Playwright fundamentals with the "why"

Why this lesson is here. The plan points this phase at the basics module, then at a Selenium rewrite. The exit is the actionability list.

Study: everything in section 1, plus locator operators, expect.poll, expect.toPass, soft assertions, expect.configure and custom matchers.

Do: rewrite a Selenium test you know well in Playwright and list every explicit wait you deleted and why. Break strict mode on purpose, read the error, fix it three ways, and decide which you would defend.

Exit: list the actionability checks unaided and explain what "stable" means for an animating element.
