Phase 3 — framework architecture

Why this lesson is here. Architecture comes after fundamentals. Its exercise is the proof project in the last module.

Study: fixture scopes, auto, option, fixture timeouts, box, teardown order, mergeTests; page objects as fixture-supplied collaborators; projects, dependencies and teardown; storage-state authentication; timeouts; test-data strategy; tags and annotations mapped to Xray.

Do: build the proof project in section 8.

Exit: draw the framework layering on a whiteboard in under two minutes and justify each seam.
