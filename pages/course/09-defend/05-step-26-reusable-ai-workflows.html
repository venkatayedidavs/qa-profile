Step 26: Reusable AI workflows

Progress is saved in this browser.

0%

Why this lesson is here. Reusable prompts and skills, after CI and evidence. The page says to keep a plain prompt if skills are unavailable.

Outcome: reuse without unlimited authority. Prompts give task context; instructions give conventions; agents need tool boundaries.

Study: instructions, skills, test agents.

Workflow template: list inputs; separate facts/observations/hypotheses; identify oracle and missing evidence; propose one discriminating check; stop for human review. No assertion weakening, skips, arbitrary sleeps, or publication.

Optional: separate branch, compatible versions, npx playwright init-agents --loop=vscode. Review plans before generation, then every diff. Healers may suggest skips: reject those as repairs. Local-only access, bounded attempts; regenerate definitions after upgrades.

Pass when: reviewed reuse, not merely installed agents/files.
