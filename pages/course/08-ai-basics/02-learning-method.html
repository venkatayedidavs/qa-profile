Learning method

Why this lesson is here. This method explicitly follows the Quality Assurance loop, so it sits after Part 1 and before the AI exercises.

  1. Understand: explain the concept in your own words.
  2. Predict: write the expected behavior before asking AI.
  3. Practice: make one small change and observe what happens.
  4. Verify: compare the result with a requirement, a test, or the official documentation.
  5. Prove: complete the independent check without an AI-written answer.

This follows the QA course's learning method. Completion means you can explain and reproduce a result, not that an assistant said it worked.

Use a separate local practice folder, not this resume workspace or an employer repository. Use synthetic data only. Reading and local checks need no model subscription; live Copilot exercises depend on your account, usage allowance, editor version, and organization policy. Never paste secrets or private records into chat.
