2.9 Scenario and whiteboard

Why this lesson is here. Whiteboard scenarios come after you have a spine for the shorter questions.

Expect at least one of these. Practise them out loud.

  • Design an automation strategy for a customer-onboarding flow with identity verification, document upload and an external screening call.
  • You inherit a suite with four hundred tests, a forty per cent flake rate and a ninety-minute run. What do you do first?
  • The team wants to run end-to-end tests against production. Argue both sides, then propose a safe subset.
  • Design tests for a file upload with a progress bar and an asynchronous virus-scan result.
  • A page shows data from three services; one is slow and sometimes times out. Make the test deterministic without hiding the real risk.
