Interview question bank

The study page collapses every answer so you can speak first and then compare. Phase 5 of the plan uses this bank for the mock interviews.

Why this module is here. The study page collapses every answer so you can speak first and then compare. Phase 5 of the plan uses this bank for the mock interviews.

What it unlocks. The proof project and the rehearsal. The decisions in that project are the decisions these questions ask about.

Each block gives the question, what is actually being tested, and the spine of a strong answer. Target sixty to ninety seconds, then stop and offer to go deeper.

  1. Lesson 1 2.1 TypeScript

    The bank follows the plan's phase order. Language is the first block, matching the first exit criterion.

  2. Lesson 2 2.2 Playwright fundamentals

    Fundamentals follow the language block, matching phase 2.

  3. Lesson 3 2.3 Framework design

    Framework design follows fundamentals, matching phase 3.

  4. Lesson 4 2.4 Reliability and flakiness

    Reliability follows design, matching the trace narration phase 4 asks you to give out loud.

  5. Lesson 5 2.5 CI, scale and reporting

    Scale and reporting stay with phase 4, after you can diagnose a single failure.

  6. Lesson 6 2.6 API, network and data

    API, network, and data are the breadth phase, after isolation is clear enough to mock on purpose.

  7. Lesson 7 2.7 Accessibility, visual and emulation

    Accessibility, visual checks, and emulation are grouped with that breadth, after the core reliability questions.

  8. Lesson 8 2.8 Process, leadership and collaboration

    Process and leadership follow the technical blocks. The plan says not to answer strategy as a tool user only.

  9. Lesson 9 2.9 Scenario and whiteboard

    Whiteboard scenarios come after you have a spine for the shorter questions.

  10. Lesson 10 2.10 Live coding drills

    Live coding applies the earlier blocks. The plan says to practise until you can do it without the docs.

  11. Lesson 11 2.11 Questions to ask them

    The bank ends here, as the original does. Asking is part of the interview, after you can answer.
