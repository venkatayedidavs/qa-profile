10. Handling the experience gap honestly

Why this lesson is here. How to state scope honestly. The plan places this after the red-flag phrases.

You will be asked how long you have used Playwright. Prepare one calm, truthful sentence followed by evidence of depth.

  1. State the real scope of your Playwright work plainly.
  2. Immediately give a concrete artefact: the framework, what it covers, how it runs in the pipeline, and one hard problem you solved in it.
  3. Bridge to the longer arc: nine years of test design, risk analysis, test management and API testing, plus Selenium and Cypress, which is why the tooling transfer was fast.
  4. Close with current learning, named specifically — aria snapshots, sharded reporting. Specific curiosity reads as genuine; "I love learning new things" does not.

Never claim a tool, version or feature you have not touched. If you have read about it but not used it, say exactly that and then describe how you would apply it. Interviewers respect that far more than a bluff that collapses on the follow-up question.

Voiceover script

Read this aloud if you want the spoken pass. The course does not include a recording. The same words are in the script file next to this lesson.

When someone asks how long you have used Playwright, use four beats, and keep every beat true to your own work.

First, state the real scope in one calm sentence. Not the scope you wish you had.

Second, give a concrete artefact straight away. The framework, what it covers, how it runs in the pipeline, and one hard problem you actually solved in it.

Third, bridge to the longer arc. Test design, risk, test management, API testing, and the other tools you have shipped are why a transfer can be fast. Use your arc.

Fourth, close on learning that has a name. Aria snapshots, sharded reporting, or whatever you are actually practising. Specific curiosity is credible. Saying you love learning new things is not.

If you have read about a feature and have not used it, say exactly that, then say how you would apply it. Do not claim the tool, the version, or the feature.

This plan's written example mentions nine years and a particular resume. That example is not your biography. The four beats are what you reuse.
