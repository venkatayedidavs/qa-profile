## Meeting Route

**Meeting:** 17 September 2026, with HR, manager and union representative.

**First preference:** Stay employed at Resurs in suitable work.

- **No matching role:** Full profile -> Reassess all vacancies -> Consultant work.
- **Matching role:** Next steps -> If still competing, assess remaining vacancies
  too -> Consultant work while no suitable role is secured.
- **Still doubts about development ability:** Offer a one-month internal
  assessment as a last option, then agree written next steps.

Use one step at a time. Pause for answers. Do not lead with severance.

## 1. Hear Their Message

**If they say:** "QA roles are being removed, including yours. You will receive
three months' notice with normal salary, but no severance."

Let them finish. Then say:

> "I hear what you are saying. I would like to take one step back. My first
> preference is to remain working at Resurs, possibly in another role.
> My current title may not show my full technical background, so I would like
> to make sure that background is included in the reassignment assessment."

## 2. Explain Your Full Profile

> "I have a bachelor's degree in Information Technology, which gave me a
> technical foundation in software development. I have around nine years of
> IT experience, including three years at Resurs.
>
> My strengths include quality assurance and especially test automation,
> including programming. My current work here has been mainly manual QA, but
> my technical background extends beyond those day-to-day responsibilities.
>
> I also have AWS cloud knowledge and certification, relevant to the technology
> Resurs uses. I have studied and used AI in software development and testing
> workflows, and have related certifications. I mean AI in the SDLC and STLC,
> not only general-purpose chatbot use.
>
> I bring Resurs and banking-product knowledge. I have completed Swedish B
> and am continuing my Swedish studies.
>
> I would like this full profile considered across all available positions.
> I have reviewed the vacancies and see areas where my skills match.
> I am happy to provide evidence of my experience and certifications."

## 3. Follow Their Answer

### A. They Say No Position Matches

> "Have you already completed a reassignment assessment using my full profile?
> I would like you to reassess all current vacancies in light of the skills
> I have described, rather than limit the assessment to my QA Engineer title.
>
> Please share the assessment with me and my union representative, or provide
> a written explanation covering the positions reviewed, their requirements,
> which of my skills were considered, and the reasons for each conclusion.
>
> Where there is a gap, what specifically is missing, and could reasonable
> onboarding or training address it?"

**If they say the full profile was already considered:**

> "Thank you. Please walk me and my union representative through that
> assessment and share the explanation in writing, so we can understand it."

**Next:** go to step 4. Do not limit the request to one named vacancy.

### B. They Say a Position Matches

> "Thank you, I appreciate that you have identified a role that matches my
> profile. I am interested. What next steps and timeline do you have in mind?
> Is this a reassignment offer, or is there still a selection process?"

**If they say other people are applying, or you must apply:**

> "I understand that other people are being considered. I remain interested
> and am willing to participate. What criteria will be used, and how is this
> being handled as part of the reassignment process?
>
> While that is unresolved, please also assess the remaining vacancies.
> My profile includes development-related skills through test automation,
> AWS knowledge, AI in development and testing, and banking-product knowledge.
> Please consider those skills, not only my current QA title.
>
> I am happy to provide supporting documentation. Please share the complete
> assessment and explain the matches, gaps and onboarding considered."

**Next:** go to step 4 while no suitable role has been secured.

**If they make a concrete reassignment offer:**

> "I would like to consider the offer. Please provide the duties, salary,
> location, hours, treatment of seniority and benefits, and response deadline
> in writing so I can review it promptly with my union."

**Next:** review the offer with the union. Do not let its deadline pass while
waiting for another assessment. Go to step 6 to confirm written next steps.

## 4. Second Option: Consultant Work

Raise this after the vacancy discussion, in either branch where no suitable
role is secured. The request is to do suitable work as a Resurs employee,
not to become a consultant.

> "My first preference is reassignment to an available employee position.
> I would also like you to review the IT work currently performed by
> consultants: what roles they perform, what skills are needed, and how those
> requirements compare with my skills.
>
> Could I take on suitable work currently handled by a consultant and remain
> employed at Resurs? Where my skills partly match, please consider the gap
> and whether reasonable onboarding could address it.
>
> My automation experience involves programming. I am confident I can
> contribute to suitable development work, and I would welcome an assessment
> against the actual responsibilities. I can provide relevant examples.
> Please explain which alternatives were considered and the outcome."

## 5. Last Option: One-Month Internal Assessment

**Use only if they still doubt your ability to perform identified development
work.** Do not offer this before asking them to assess vacancies and consultant
work. Do not offer to give up your existing employment rights.

> "If your concern is whether I can perform that development work, would you
> consider a one-month internal assignment with agreed tasks, support and
> review criteria, so I can demonstrate my ability?
>
> I am proposing this within my existing employment, with salary, seniority
> and benefits preserved, not as a new probationary contract. I would like
> the arrangement and what happens after the review agreed in writing with
> my union representative."

**If they decline:** ask for their reasons, then go to step 6.

## 6. Close With Written Next Steps

> "I acknowledge receiving the information. I am not agreeing to a voluntary
> termination or settlement today. My first preference remains to stay.
> Please provide the assessment explanation and any proposal so I can review
> them with my union representative.
>
> When will you provide the documents? Please confirm any response deadlines
> and a follow-up meeting date in writing."

**If no employment alternative is available after review:**

> "I would then like to discuss additional severance on top of my applicable
> notice entitlement, or a longer paid notice period, including the treatment
> of pension contributions and benefits."

**Before leaving:** clarify whether formal notice was served. Keep a copy and
record the date. Ask for written grounds under LAS section 9 if terminated.
Note the document owner, delivery date, response deadlines and follow-up date.
Send a factual email recap afterwards, copying the union representative.

## Private Notes: Not Spoken

- **Preparation:** brief the union before the meeting. Confirm the collective
  agreement, notice entitlement and previous negotiations. Bring the vacancy
  descriptions, certification details and examples you are permitted to share.
- **Skills:** distinguish academic development, automation programming, cloud
  knowledge and actual work experience. These support assessment, not a claim
  that every developer role is equivalent to test automation. Name only AI
  uses and certifications you can explain. No specific Swedish C/D course is
  stated here because the current course was unclear.
- **LAS section 7:** the reassignment obligation concerns suitable available
  work with the employer. A partial match does not guarantee a position or
  unlimited retraining. Request the full assessment; do not assert an
  unrestricted personal right to every internal document. Let the union
  explain how LAS and the applicable agreement apply.
- **LAS section 9:** grounds for termination must be provided in writing when
  the employee requests this. The union can seek relevant information under
  the applicable MBL rules, including sections 15, 18 and 19.
- **Consultants:** there is no blanket employee right to take a consultant's
  assignment. This is a proposal for the employer and union to examine.
- **One-month proposal:** optional and subject to written agreement, not a
  statutory entitlement. A trial assignment does not automatically cancel or
  extend any notice already served. Have the union check its effect first.
- **Deadlines:** receiving notice does not require agreement or a signature.
  Asking for reassessment or documents does not pause notice or legal deadlines.
  The ordinary LAS section 40 deadline to notify an intention to challenge
  validity is two weeks, subject to exceptions and applicable agreement rules.
  Ask the union to check immediately; this script is not a formal challenge.
- **Review before signing:** have the union review any settlement, including
  rights waived, pension and benefits; check unemployment-benefit consequences
  with the relevant a-kassa. Extra severance or longer notice is not automatic.
  Do not reject a reassignment offer or miss its deadline without union advice.
