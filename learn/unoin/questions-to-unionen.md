## Opening

> "This situation is new to me, and I have basic questions. Could you please
> explain the process, your role, my rights under the collective agreement and
> law, and what I should do before signing or responding to anything?"

## Redundancy Process

- What is the redundancy process, step by step?
- What does "arbetsbrist" mean in my case?
- Has Resurs completed the required negotiation with Unionen?
- Must Resurs first assess other suitable jobs for me?
- Will my full experience, education and technical skills be considered?
- What documents can I request?

## The Coming Meeting

- What should I expect in the meeting with HR, my manager and Unionen?
- Can I ask questions and request time to think?

## Unionen's Role

- What is Unionen's role in this meeting?
- Can you speak on my behalf if necessary?
- Can you challenge or question the employer's reassignment assessment?
- Can you request more information from Resurs?
- Who should I contact at Unionen after the meeting?

## Possible Alternatives

- Can consultant work be considered as an alternative for employees affected by
  the redundancy process when their skills match the work?

## Challenging the Decision

- What are my rights if I challenge the decision or do not agree with it?
- What happens if I do not agree with the reassignment assessment or termination?
- What deadlines apply, and what must Unionen or I do to protect my rights?
- What should I avoid signing before Unionen has reviewed it?

## Severance Pay

- What is severance pay?
- Is severance pay separate from my normal salary during the notice period?
- Is severance automatic, or must it be negotiated?
- Could severance or a settlement affect my a-kassa, pension, benefits or tax?
- Can I take any settlement or termination agreement away for review before
  signing it?

## Documents to Prepare

Ask Unionen whether they need copies of:

- Employment contract.
- Collective agreement name, if known.
- Any redundancy or termination letter.
- Notice-period information.
- Vacancy descriptions.
- Relevant certificates and qualifications.
- Emails or meeting invitations from Resurs.
- Details of employment dates and current salary.
